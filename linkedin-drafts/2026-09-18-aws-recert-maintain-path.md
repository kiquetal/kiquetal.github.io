## English Version (expanded)

Done ✅ — my AWS Solutions Architect – Professional credential is current for another year, and I did it without blocking out a weekend for the 3-hour exam. I used Skill Builder's newer **Maintain** path. 🎓

AWS certifications are valid for 3 years, with two ways to keep them current:

🔁 **Renew** — pass the latest version of the exam → **+3 years**
🧩 **Maintain** — complete curated training + hands-on labs on AWS Skill Builder → **+1 year**

I chose **Maintain** — and here's the honest version, not the tidy one:

I *planned* for 800+ points and four SimuLearn labs. I actually finished at **740/700 with two labs**. What closed the gap was a course I'd completed way back in **2022** (Deploying Serverless Applications, 120 pts) that still counted. So the practical lesson: the 700-point total is the only binding constraint, and **prior eligible completions count** — check what you've already done before planning a whole new route. Mine saved me two labs.

A few things that are genuinely easy to miss:
• Eligibility opens **within 90 days of expiry — and only while the cert is still active.** Let it lapse and Maintain is off the table.
• You need an active paid Skill Builder subscription.
• Maintaining a higher cert also **extends a still-active related lower-level cert** to the new date.

On intent: Maintain buys me **one year (now valid to 2027-09-24)** — it's not me dodging the exam. I still plan to sit the real **SAP-C02** in 2027. I did this to stay current *and* keep learning in the same window, and for learning it was well worth it. The hands-on SimuLearn labs — fixing a deliberately broken live environment (VPC routing conflicts, inter-region Transit Gateway peering) — were far more useful than watching videos. A nice experience I'd recommend.

I documented the whole thing publicly: how the path works, the point math, and per-course notes with diagrams (built alongside Kiro). One source of truth, updated as I completed each course.
